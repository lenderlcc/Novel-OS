Natural prose comes from purposeful specificity, character voice, responsive rhythm and real interaction. Naturalness is not literary restraint, slow pacing, subtle everything, minimal dialogue or large omissions. Follow approved project preferences within hard chapter intent. Fast, direct, high-density prose can be natural; clear emotional payoff need not explain emotions repeatedly. AS-01 Over Explanation: if action or dialogue already reveals something, avoid narratorial repetition of its meaning. AS-02 Emotion Labeling: favor behavior, reactions, choices, speech and timing over repeated declarations such as “他感到愤怒” or “她感到悲伤”; use direct emotion when it is the precise choice. AS-12 Over-signposting: avoid continually announcing “这意味着…”“他不知道的是…”“真正的问题在于…”; let the text establish the connection.
Together with AS-03/04/05/06/07, these are flexible editorial guidance, never sentence-length formulas, obligatory paragraph actions, dialogue quotas or a forbidden-word engine. Do not create artificial errors, typos, broken grammar, random sentence damage or mechanical synonym swaps. Do not optimize for evading an AI detector. Build the project's own style; do not copy a real author's prose as a style template. Preserve intelligibility and narrative purpose over cosmetic variation.
AS-03 Uniform Rhythm: vary sentences, paragraphs and scene movement in response to dramatic pressure, not by a numeric pattern. AS-04 Template Transition: connect scenes through continuing action or consequence instead of repeated stock transition phrases. AS-05 Dialogue Functionalization: preserve interaction, hesitation, relationships and hidden intentions; conversation is more than information delivery. AS-06 Character Voice Collapse: preserve distinct character voices and context-supported differences. AS-07 Excessive Closure: leave room for resonance and unresolved emotion instead of explaining and closing every scene. These choices serve the scene; they impose no quotas.

LOW literary_ornamentation does not permit bad grammar or broken language. A target audience never mandates short sentences, colloquialism, cliffhangers, reversals, face-slapping or 爽点. Do not require a conflict every 300 words or dialogue in every paragraph. Do not equate unresolved story questions with weak reader payoff.
