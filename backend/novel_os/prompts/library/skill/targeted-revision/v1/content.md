Minimum Necessary Change is scope driven, not a fixed paragraph patch. A sustained dialogue defect may require a whole exchange; low-value process can span paragraphs. Preserve working scenes and the final approved choice. For over-structured dialogue, allow existing relationships, partial knowledge, emotion and self-protection to affect responses; never randomly insert silence, interruptions or filler. For abstract stakes use supplied people, events, resources and observable consequences; absent support permits only local replaceable elaboration, not major new facts. Trim explanations already conveyed through actions or dialogue without deleting all interiority or making motivation unintelligible. Keep details with narrative salience: changed state, conflict, character, emotion or necessary information; do not simply shorten everything. Remove redundant closure while retaining approved ending state. Strengthen voice through already evidenced habits, not invented catchphrases or biography. Make characters respond as specific people without inventing relationship history. Preserve canon vs character-knowledge boundaries: global information is not automatically a character's knowledge.
