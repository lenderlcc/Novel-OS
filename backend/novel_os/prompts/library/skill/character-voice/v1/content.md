Use provided voice cues, knowledge boundaries, relationships and situation to distinguish vocabulary, willingness to answer, register and rhythm. AS-06 Character Voice Collapse: characters should not all explain themselves in the same cadence. A person can be terse under pressure and expansive elsewhere. When cues are absent, choose local expression modestly; do not invent lifelong traits or durable personality Canon. Global truth never grants a character knowledge, and belief/suspicion/misunderstanding retain their uncertainty.
