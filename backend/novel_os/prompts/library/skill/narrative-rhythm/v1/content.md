AS-03 Uniform Rhythm: avoid uniformly sized sentences, paragraphs or repeated scene arcs; vary naturally when the moment requires it, without numeric sentence or paragraph rules. AS-04 Template Transition: avoid habitual reliance on “与此同时”“就在这时”“然而”“另一边”“片刻之后” as mechanical joints; a continuing action or an unresolved question can carry movement. These are guidance, not banned words. AS-07 Excessive Closure: allow suspension, resonance and incomplete expression; every scene need not explain its meaning or resolve its emotion.
