Perform semantic decomposition before producing the CreativeBrief. This is a model task; do not use a single-token lookup rule and do not assign an entire multi-intent request to MUST.

1. Read the complete input and identify its meaningful propositions, including contrasts, negation, conditions, timing, examples and permissions. A sentence may contain a positive goal and a separate prohibition. Keep their relationship, but represent their different force separately.
2. MUST contains explicit required outcomes/actions, including an unambiguously stated core goal without the word "must". FORBIDDEN contains explicit constraints on what must not happen, including prohibited end states. SHOULD contains desired but negotiable execution guidance. PREFERENCE contains optional choices or stylistic/narrative suggestions, not mandatory events. PRESERVE retains explicitly requested existing elements. Existing structured REQUIREMENT sources keep their server-owned category; never reinterpret them.
3. Words such as "希望", "可以", "不要", "必须", "尽量", "if appropriate" and "may" are contextual cues, not a classifier. "I hope the core goal is achieved" may still state the essential goal. A quoted hypothetical is not a command. An optional example must not become a required event. Do not invent a preference merely to populate an empty array. A category with no evidence stays empty.
4. Permission to choose concrete settings, local events, dialogue or delivery belongs to creative_freedom, not MUST. An optional desired narrative effect can be PREFERENCE. Do not equate local freedom with permission to change plot direction, character identity, constraints or locked facts.
5. For TASK_INPUT and DECISION constraints, choose the smallest exact contiguous source span that proves one proposition with its force and scope. Keep negation, conditions, "only", exceptions, optionality and necessary timing/subject context. Never quote an entire paragraph to support an unrelated short item, and never shorten a quote until it reverses the meaning. text must equal quote exactly. Overlapping evidence is allowed when scopes genuinely overlap, but avoid duplicate constraints.
6. Selected structured REQUIREMENT items are a separate case: retain every item's full content, including qualifiers, once in its original category. Their atomicity is already user-owned; do not split, shorten, relabel, normalize whitespace or drop them to satisfy the minimal-span guidance for raw input.
7. Derive creative_freedom from the granted scope. LOW means local choices are substantially specified; MEDIUM means ordinary execution detail is open; HIGH requires clear delegation of substantial local invention. State what is open and what remains fixed. Select only justified allowed_operations: LOCAL_DETAIL, SCENE_ORDER, INFORMATION_DELIVERY, LOCAL_CONFLICT. SCENE_ORDER cannot override explicit order or temporal functions. HIGH never expands authority; major_changes is always PROPOSAL_ONLY. Unspecified local detail alone is not an invented new requirement.
8. Separate intent_summary (why/experience), chapter_objective (what changes), required_outcome (what must hold at the end) and desired_reader_effect (what readers feel/infer). These summaries may paraphrase; literal constraint text/quote may not. Do not fabricate names, settings, dialogue or specific events during normalization.
9. Confidence is a self-assessment of semantic interpretation, not schema validity, fluency, execution success or approval authority. Do not copy a default 0.95. Assess coverage, category force, evidence scope, conflicts and unresolved ambiguity. Rough reporting bands: 0.85–0.95 for a clearly grounded interpretation; 0.60–0.84 when meaningful interpretation remains uncertain but safe; below 0.60 for substantial unresolved uncertainty. These are reporting conventions, not measured probabilities. Do not report certainty when consequential assumptions remain. For an unresolved HIGH-impact ambiguity, overall confidence must not exceed confidence in that unresolved interpretation. Use separate assumption/ambiguity confidence and impact honestly; explicit creative freedom is not low confidence.
10. Before returning, compare each source proposition with the brief: required outcomes retained; negatives isolated; recommendations not promoted; options remain optional; creative permissions bounded; literal evidence sufficient and minimal; summary fields distinct. Put inferred assumptions outside user constraint arrays. Preserve conflicts visibly instead of silently resolving them.
