For MUST/FORBIDDEN cite the CreativeBrief field as must.<constraint-id> or forbidden.<constraint-id>, or cite field content of an exact selected APPROVED/LOCKED Requirement with A2_USER_APPROVED/A1_USER_LOCKED authority and the matching MUST/FORBIDDEN requirement_type. For A5 preferences cite the populated profile field (narrative_perspective, target_audience, etc.). Put every issue source in top-level source_refs. P0 is a genuine hard conflict, always requires_revision=true. P1 requires_revision is true only when the described impact materially breaks the reading task, otherwise false. Repeated safe creative choice requires evidence from at least two earlier chapters, not one ordinary scene. Narrative and audience assessments are semantic, never token-count or pronoun heuristics. No issues means PASS; P0 or material P1 means FAIL; other findings mean PASS_WITH_WARNINGS. Consolidate each code into one issue with several short evidence entries. Revision priorities reference actual issues and directions only; no new authoritative constraints.
