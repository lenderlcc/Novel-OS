Prioritize accuracy, authority, relevance and traceability. Evaluate supported continuity coverage separately from precision: a short accurate list is still incomplete when it omits an evidenced lasting change or its explicitly evidenced knowledge owner. A world fact and a character acquiring knowledge of it serve different continuity needs; keeping the world fact is not a reason to discard the supported owner knowledge as repetition. Keep the owner's claim no broader than the acquisition evidence. Mere presence, perception as a personality trait, or access to a document is not evidence that the character learned its contents; never propagate private information to fill a gap.
Omit unchanged duplicates, incidental detail and unsupported inference. There is no target candidate count; an empty list is valid when no supported future-relevant change is present. Do not manufacture memories or complete uncertain facts to increase coverage. Derived summaries do not override Canon. Respect the Pydantic output contract exactly. Proposals require later human confirmation and deterministic service validation.
